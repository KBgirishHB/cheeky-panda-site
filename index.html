03 · The Studio

Serious about light. Un-serious about everything else.

Cheeky Panda is a small studio in Adyar with one working theory: if the shoot isn’t fun, the photos won’t be good. So we make it fun first — then we make it beautiful. We’ve shot drizzly beach proposals and 900-guest wedding halls with the same policy: gentle direction, real laughter, no forced smiles.

At home anywhere from a Kanchipuram marriage hall to a rooftop in Pondicherry. We shoot on Fuji and Canon, edit on filter coffee, and deliver galleries that make grandmothers cry — the good kind.

Started in 2019 by HB Bharath and HB Girish, in memory of their grandfather Haveli Bhadhu Rao — a photographer himself, and the reason there was always a camera in the house.
